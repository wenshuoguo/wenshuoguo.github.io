UC Berkeley

I was a co-president for Women in Computer Science and Electrical Engineering (WICSE) at Berkeley from 2021-2022. I also served as the social chair for WICSE in 2020-2021. During my study at Berkeley I was a Berkeley AI Research (BAIR) mentor.

Women in Machine Learning (WiML)

I served as the program chair for the 2nd WiML Un-Workshop co-located with the ICML conference. This technical workshop gives female faculty, research scientists, and graduate students in the machine learning community an opportunity to meet, network and exchange ideas, participate in career-focused panel discussions with senior women in industry and academia and learn from each other.

Program committee / reviewers for conferences

I am a program committee member for the 33rd, 34th International Conference on Algorithmic Learning Theory (ALT 2022, 2023). I have been a reviewer for these conferences / journals: NeurIPS, ICML, ICLR, CVPR, AISTATS, ALT, TMLR.
